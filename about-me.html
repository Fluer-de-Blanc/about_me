About Me

Who I Am

Hi! I'm a Jam a college student who spends most of my free time playing video games, from fast-paced shooters to relaxing story-driven RPGs. Gaming isn't just a way to pass the time for me it's where I unwind, think strategically, and connect with friends online.

Aside from gaming, I enjoy learning new things about software design, which is actually part of why I'm building this very page. I believe that a hobby, when managed well, can make a student sharper, calmer, and more motivated rather than being a distraction.

A Message to 4th Year Students Studying Real Hard

To every 4th year student grinding through readings, requirements, and exam preparation: you don't have to give up gaming completely to finish strong. A short, timed gaming session can actually recharge your focus after hours of studying real hard.

The key is balance treat your game time like a reward after reaching a study goal, not an escape from your responsibilities. Finish the quest in real life first, then enjoy the one on screen.
